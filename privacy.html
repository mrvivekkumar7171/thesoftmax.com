1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how TheSoftMax ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our applications and website at thesoftmax.com.

TheSoftMax builds entirely serverless applications — software that runs directly on your device without any backend server infrastructure. This architectural choice means that we are technically incapable of collecting your personal data in the traditional sense, because there are no servers to send data to.

This policy applies to all TheSoftMax products including the Music Player and any future applications.

2 Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. More specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • We do not collect location data, IP addresses, or device identifiers
  • We do not run analytics or user behavior tracking of any kind
  • We do not require account creation or registration
  • We do not collect payment information (all our current software is free)

There is no mechanism in our applications to transmit data to TheSoftMax — we have no server endpoints to receive it.

3 Local Storage on Your Device

Some of our applications use your browser's localStorage API to store preferences locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to anyone. Examples include:

  • Music Player: Volume level, shuffle preference, repeat preference, and first-visit flag are stored locally so your settings persist between sessions

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or localStorage via your browser settings. Doing so will reset any saved preferences to their defaults.

Key point: localStorage data is stored only on your device. It is not transmitted, synced, or accessible to TheSoftMax or any third party.

4 Third-Party Services

Our applications may interact with third-party services to deliver content or functionality. These interactions are initiated by your device directly and are subject to each service's own privacy policy:

TheSoftMax has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, the content, privacy practices, or policies of any third-party services.

5 Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child is using our services, please note that no data is collected or transmitted. If you have concerns, please contact us.

6 Hosting & Infrastructure

TheSoftMax applications are hosted exclusively on GitHub Pages, a static site hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. We do not operate, own, or control any server infrastructure.

Because our applications consist solely of static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files:

  • There is no application server processing your requests
  • There is no database storing information about you
  • There is no backend API receiving data from you
  • All computation occurs locally on your device

7 Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, many traditional data rights (right to access, right to erasure, data portability) are not applicable in the usual sense. However:

  • Local data control: Any data stored locally on your device (via localStorage) is entirely under your control. You can delete it at any time through your browser settings.
  • No account to delete: There are no accounts, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.
  • GDPR / CCPA: As we collect no personal data and have no servers, we are not a data controller or data processor under GDPR or CCPA. These regulations do not impose obligations on us with respect to your data.

If you are in the EU, UK, California, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy laws and have concerns about how third-party services (like GitHub Pages or Google Fonts) handle your data, please review their respective privacy policies.

8 Data Security

Since we collect and store no personal data on any server, there is no centralized database of user information that could be breached.

Our website is served over HTTPS, ensuring that the files your browser downloads are transmitted securely. Local data stored in your browser is subject to your device's security and browser sandbox protections.

We recommend keeping your browser and operating system updated for the best security posture.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our applications or to clarify our practices. When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our applications after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
