Privacy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Transparent, flexible, and built on user respect. Learn how our software handles your data, analytics, accounts, and privacy rights.

Last updated: November 2026 Compliant Worldwide (GDPR, CCPA, India DPDP, Google Play)

1 Overview & Scope

At TheSoftMax ("we," "us," or "our"), we design and publish high-quality mobile applications, games, utility tools, and web software with data privacy, transparency, and user autonomy as core engineering principles.

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications, games, software, and services published by TheSoftMax, including our products distributed on the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and our official website at thesoftmax.com.

We build software for a diverse catalog ranging from offline utilities and hardware diagnostics to interactive casual games and productivity tools. Because our catalog serves varied use cases, this Privacy Policy establishes a clear, transparent, and modular framework that covers all our applications — whether an application is used entirely offline without an account, offers optional profile synchronization, or features account registration.

WE DO NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE YOUR PERSONAL DATA TO THIRD PARTIES.

2 Core Privacy Principles

  • Data Minimization: We process only the minimum technical data necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and monetize our software.
  • Offline-First Architecture: Wherever feasible, our applications are designed to operate locally on your device without requiring mandatory internet connectivity or cloud accounts.
  • User Choice & Control: When account creation or analytics features are offered, users retain full autonomy to use guest mode, adjust privacy settings, manage advertising consent, or permanently delete their account and associated data.
  • Transparency: We explicitly disclose all SDKs, diagnostic tools, and ad partners used across our product line.

3 Information We May Process & Collect

Depending on the specific application you install and the optional features you choose to interact with, we or our integrated third-party service providers may process the following categories of information:

A. Device, Hardware & System Telemetry

  • Device hardware specifications: Manufacturer, device model, board, CPU architecture, core count, clock frequencies, total RAM, internal storage capacity, display resolution, refresh rate, and battery charging metrics.
  • Operating system details: Android OS version, API level, security patch level, system build fingerprint, and locale/language settings.
  • Network connectivity state: Network type (Wi-Fi, cellular, or offline) and coarse IP-derived geographic location (country and city level) used for regional settings, localized content, and ad frequency management.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): A resettable advertising identifier provided by Google Play Services, used strictly for ad delivery, attribution, and privacy consent management in ad-supported applications.

B. On-Device Hardware & Sensor Diagnostics (Utility & Diagnostic Applications)

For applications designed to test and evaluate hardware sensors (such as Sensor Master):

  • Real-time diagnostic data: Measurements from physical sensors including tri-axial accelerometer (linear acceleration, device tilt), gyroscope (angular velocity, orientation), magnetometer (ambient geomagnetic field, compass heading), ambient light sensor (lux levels), infrared proximity sensor (distance events), biometric fingerprint scanner availability, NFC transceiver state, and GNSS/GPS satellite constellation availability.
  • Acoustic & Optical testing: Real-time sound pressure decibel levels captured via the device microphone, camera sensor capabilities and optical viewfinder preview, and speaker acoustic frequency sweeps.
  • ON-DEVICE PRIVACY COMMITMENT: All hardware and sensor diagnostic tests are executed strictly on your physical device in real time. We DO NOT record, transmit, stream, or store your camera images, microphone audio samples, biometric fingerprint data, or precise GPS travel routes on any remote server. Once a diagnostic test finishes, raw sensor measurements are immediately discarded from memory.

C. User Profile & Account Credentials (Only for Apps with Optional/Required Login)

  • For applications that support user accounts (e.g., for cross-device cloud saves, multiplayer leaderboards, or profile backups), we process basic identifiers such as your display name, email address, avatar photo, and a unique user identifier provided via Google Sign-In, email, or third-party OAuth authentication.
  • If an application does not offer account registration or is operated in Guest Mode, NO personal profile information, name, or email address is ever requested, processed, or stored.

D. Local Preferences & Session State

  • Application configurations: Selected language, dark/light theme preference, sound effects volume, haptic vibration intensity, notification reminder schedules, and high scores/levels completed.
  • Storage location: Stored securely in local device memory (via Android SharedPreferences, Jetpack DataStore, or local SQLite database). This data remains entirely on your device and is never uploaded to external servers unless you explicitly choose to log into a cloud account.

E. Diagnostic Telemetry & Crash Reporting

  • Technical crash dumps: Stack traces, exception types, crash timestamps, active activity/screen name, memory usage at the time of failure, and thread states to identify and resolve stability issues across device models.
  • Breadcrumbs: Chronological technical milestones (e.g., screen navigation, diagnostic test started/completed, memory optimization triggered) captured by Sentry to diagnose what triggered an unexpected shutdown.
  • Crash reports contain NO personal conversations, contacts, gallery photos, passwords, or sensitive user files.

F. User Feedback & Support Correspondence

  • When you submit a bug report, troubleshooting inquiry, or feedback form (either in-app or via our website), we receive your message, feedback category, and any optional contact details (such as your name or email) that you provide.
  • If you optionally attach a screenshot to a bug report, it is transmitted securely and reviewed solely for technical debugging purposes.

4 Sensitive Permissions & In-App Prominent Disclosures

Certain advanced features require system-level Android permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required only when you run camera sensor diagnostics or preview viewfinder tests.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Required only when evaluating real-time ambient sound levels or testing audio recording fidelity.
  • Location (android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required only when analyzing GNSS satellite constellations and GPS accuracy.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required only to deliver routine maintenance check reminders or important service alerts.

Prominent Disclosure Policy: In strict accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, our applications display a Prominent In-App Disclosure dialog immediately before the system permission dialog is triggered. This disclosure specifically explains why the permission is needed and confirms that the data is processed on-device and never sold or shared. Permission is requested only at the exact moment you choose to initiate that specific feature, never on app launch. You may grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device Settings.

5 User Accounts, Guest Mode & Account Deletion Rights

We believe user accounts should empower you, not restrict you:

A. Guest Mode & Accountless Use

Where available, our applications are fully usable without account creation. You can complete tests, play gameplay levels, track progress, and customize settings entirely as a guest without providing an email address or personal credentials.

B. In-App Account Deletion Facility (Google Play Compliant)

In compliance with Google Play Store Account Deletion requirements and global data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP), any user who registers an account has the direct facility to permanently delete their account:

  1. Open the application's Settings or Profile screen.
  2. Select "Delete Account" or "Delete Profile".
  3. Confirm your selection.

Confirming deletion permanently purges your user credentials, cloud profile, synchronized settings, and cloud identifiers from our servers. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

C. Web-Based Account Deletion Request

You may also request account and data deletion online without reinstalling the application:

  • Visit our official web contact form: thesoftmax.com/contact
  • Select the inquiry category: "Account & Data Deletion"
  • Submit your registered email address and application name.

Verified requests are processed and confirmed promptly within 30 days.

D. Local Device Data Deletion

For applications operating in guest mode or without accounts, your data is stored solely on your hardware. You can permanently erase all local data at any moment by navigating to:

Android Settings → Apps → [Application Name] → Storage → "Clear Storage / Clear Data"

or by uninstalling the application.

6 Third-Party SDKs & Service Integrations

To deliver reliable performance, analytics, secure cloud sync, and monetization, our applications integrate trusted industry-standard SDKs:

A. Google Play Services & Google Play Billing

  • Purpose: Runtime Android system libraries, biometric authentication, and secure processing of digital in-app purchases (e.g., lifetime Remove Ads).
  • We do not receive, process, or store your payment card numbers or banking credentials; all financial transactions are handled securely by Google.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

B. Google Firebase (Google LLC)

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage metrics (such as feature launch frequency and session duration) to help us understand product adoption and improve software stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) & In-App Messaging (FIAM): Delivers service notifications, tips, and maintenance notices. Generates a pseudonymous installation token that is never linked to your personal real-world identity.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Manages server-side configuration parameters, feature flags, and instant safety remote config (allowing us to pause unstable or buggy modules without requiring a full app store update).
  • Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy Support

C. Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)

  • Purpose: Real-time crash diagnostics, error stack trace logging, application freeze monitoring, and user bug report routing with optional screenshots.
  • Privacy Policy: Sentry Privacy Policy

D. Google AdMob (Google LLC)

  • Purpose: In-app advertisement serving, frequency capping, ad measurement, and fraud prevention for free versions of our applications.
  • Technologies: Uses the Google Advertising ID (AAID) and coarse IP location.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Ads Privacy

7 In-App Advertising & Consent Management

To provide our applications and tools free of charge, we display non-intrusive in-app advertisements powered by Google AdMob and certified ad networks:

A. Ad Formats

  • Banner advertisements: Displayed along the top or bottom of the screen.
  • Interstitial advertisements: Full-screen transitions shown between major logical transitions (e.g., after completing a multi-step test or level).
  • Rewarded video advertisements: Optional video ads that users voluntarily choose to watch in exchange for an in-app reward (e.g., extra game moves or daily utility access).

B. European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Switzerland (GDPR Consent)

For users located in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, our applications integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) / certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). Upon first launch in regulated regions, users are presented with a clear consent dialog offering granular control to accept personalized ads, accept non-personalized ads, or manage vendor consents. You can review and change your consent choices at any time via "Ad Privacy Choices" in the application Settings.

C. United States Privacy Regulations (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal data. For residents of California and other US states with applicable data privacy laws, ad personalization and opt-out preferences can be managed directly through the in-app privacy settings or device-level opt-out controls (Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID").

D. Ad-Free Option

Where supported, our applications offer a one-time in-app purchase (e.g., "Remove Ads") that permanently disables all banner, interstitial, and commercial ad SDKs within that application.

8 Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA, India DPDP)

Regardless of your country of residence, we respect your data privacy rights:

A. European Union & UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Legal Bases for Processing: We process data based on Consent (e.g., for ad personalization via CMP), Contractual Necessity (e.g., managing your optional account), and Legitimate Interest (e.g., fixing crashes, securing infrastructure, and preventing software abuse).
  • Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict, or erase ("Right to be Forgotten") your personal data, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time without penalty.

B. California & US State Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal data is collected, request its permanent deletion, and receive non-discriminatory service. We do not sell your personal data.

C. Indian Residents (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

Personal data is processed lawfully, transparently, and strictly for specified purposes. You retain full rights of access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal through our Data Safety contact.

9 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policies)

Our applications are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

In applications or games accessible to families, third-party advertising SDKs are configured with Child-Directed Treatment / COPPA compliance flags where applicable, restricting data collection to basic ad operations.

If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us immediately at thesoftmax.com/contact and we will take prompt steps to permanently delete such information from our systems.

10 Data Security & Retention

We implement robust administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards:

  • All data transmitted between our applications, website, and backend cloud infrastructure is protected using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Developer consoles, database clusters, and cloud APIs are guarded with strict access controls, principle of least privilege, and multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Data Retention: Local data remains on your device until you clear app cache or uninstall the software. Cloud profile data is retained only as long as your account remains active or until you request account deletion.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new application releases, feature additions, technological enhancements, or statutory regulatory updates. When changes are made, the revised policy will be published at thesoftmax.com/privacy.html with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our software following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
