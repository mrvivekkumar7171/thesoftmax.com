Privacy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Transparent, flexible, and built on user respect. Learn how our software handles your data, analytics, accounts, and privacy rights.

Last updated: October 2026 Compliant Worldwide (GDPR, CCPA, Google Play)

1 Overview & Scope

At TheSoftMax, we build high-quality mobile applications and software with privacy, transparency, and user autonomy at the forefront. We never sell your personal data. Where account login is offered, it is typically optional unless specified otherwise, and you always maintain complete control to delete your account and associated data at any time.

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications, games, software, and services provided by TheSoftMax ("we," "us," or "our"), including our Android applications, web applications, and official website at thesoftmax.com.

We believe in building transparent, reliable software. Because our catalog includes games, productivity utilities, and web tools, this policy is structured to accommodate the features of our current and future releases — including guest play, user accounts, crash reporting, and ad-supported monetization.

2 Information We May Collect & Process

Depending on which TheSoftMax application you use and which features you choose to enable, we or our integrated third-party service providers may process the following types of information:

  • Optional Profile & Account Information: If you decide to register or log into an account (e.g. via Google Sign-In, or email authentication), we process basic identifiers such as your display name, email address, avatar, and a unique user identifier to synchronize your game progress, achievements, or cross-device settings.
  • Local Gameplay & Session Data: Your high scores, current puzzle boards, streaks, audio preferences (sound, music, vibration), and dark theme settings. For guest users, this data is kept locally on your device.
  • Device & System Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, locale/language settings, network connection status, and Android Advertising ID (AAID) for ad delivery and analytics.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Telemetry: Crash timestamps, stack traces, system logs, app freeze rates, and memory state at the moment of an unexpected shutdown.
  • Feedback & Correspondence: If you submit a message via our website feedback form or email support, we receive your message and any name or email address you choose to provide.

3 User Accounts, Choice & Account Deletion Rights

We believe user accounts should empower you, not trap you:

  • Login / Guest Mode: In applications like Block Bloom (com.thesoftmax.blockbloom), account creation is completely optional. You can play all levels, enjoy full gameplay, and track your high score entirely as a guest without creating an account or providing an email address.
  • Account Deletion: In strict compliance with Google Play Store policies and global data protection standards (GDPR, CCPA), any user who creates an account has the direct facility to permanently delete their account and associated profile data:
    • In-App: Open the app settings or profile screen and select "Delete Profile / Delete Account". Confirming this action permanently deletes your cloud profile, synchronized scores, and user identifiers from our servers. Once deleted, your data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
    • Via Web Request: You may also submit an account deletion request at any time through our Contact Form (select "Account & Data Deletion") or by contacting our official support email directly at mrvivekkumar7171@gmail.com using your registered email address. We process and confirm verified deletion requests promptly upon account verification.
  • Immediate Erasure Commitment: When you delete your account, we purge your personal identifiers and cloud progress. Local scores stored on your mobile device can be cleared instantly by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

4 Crash Detection & Performance Diagnostics

To ensure our software runs smoothly, achieves consistent 60+ FPS performance, and remains free of bugs across thousands of diverse Android device models, our applications integrate industry-standard crash detection tools:

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): Collects crash diagnostics, stack traces, device OS version, and application performance metrics to identify and resolve bugs.
  • Performance Monitoring: Monitors battery efficiency, ANR (Application Not Responding) rates, and app startup latency to ensure optimal responsiveness.

For authenticated users, anonymized identifiers and email may be attached to error reports to troubleshoot account-specific issues. No passwords, game boards, or photos are transmitted.

Crash reports contain no personal conversations, photos, contacts, or sensitive data. They are technical diagnostics used strictly to maintain app stability and improve product quality.

5 In-App Advertisements

To keep our mobile games and applications free for players around the world, we display in-app advertisements powered by Google AdMob and certified ad partners. Google AdMob ads respect user consent across Europe (GDPR) and globally with consent management.

  • Ad Formats: May include non-intrusive banner ads, interstitial screens between gameplay sessions, or optional rewarded video ads (e.g. to continue a game or earn extra moves).
  • Identifiers Used: Ad networks may use the Google Advertising ID (AAID) and IP-based coarse geographic location (country or city level) to deliver, cap frequency, and measure the performance of advertisements.
  • European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Switzerland Compliance: For users located in Europe, our applications integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) / certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). Users are presented with a clear consent dialog where they can choose personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or manage partner consents in accordance with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. You can change your consent preferences at any time in the app settings.

6 Local Storage & Offline Capability

Our applications are built with an offline-first mindset:

  • Guest mode saves your data locally on your phone using Android SharedPreferences or device storage.
  • Guest mode on the web, we use browser localStorage for volume and UI settings.
  • This local data resides on your physical device. It is accessible only by the application and is never shared with third parties. You can erase all local data at any time through your Android App Settings.

7 Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our applications and website may utilize trusted third-party SDKs and service providers to support app operations. Each provider processes data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Authentication, core Android runtime libraries (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob: Ad serving, frequency capping, and ad analytics (Google Ads Privacy).
  • Supabase: User authentication, cloud database records (profiles, scores, levels), and secure avatar image storage (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • Sentry: Real-time crash diagnostics and stability telemetry (Sentry Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub Pages: Static hosting for web pages and documentation (GitHub Privacy Statement).

8 Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA, India DPDP)

Regardless of your home country, we honor your fundamental data privacy rights:

  • European Economic Area & UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): We process data on the basis of your Consent (e.g. ad consent via CMP), Contractual Necessity (e.g. maintaining your optional account), and Legitimate Interest (e.g. fixing crashes and securing our applications). You have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict processing, or erase your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten"), as well as withdraw consent at any time without penalty.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and be free from discrimination for exercising your rights. We do not sell personal information. Third-party advertising is managed via user-controlled ad identifiers and consent frameworks.
  • India (DPDP Act): We process personal data lawfully and transparently for specified purposes, respecting your rights of access, correction, and erasure.

9 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policies)

Our apps are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). In games accessible to families, we configure ad networks to request family-friendly ads (Child-Directed Treatment / COPPA flags where applicable) and do not require personal registration.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete the data.

10 Data Security & Retention

We maintain industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect information:

  • All network communications between our apps, website, and backend providers use secure TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Access to developer consoles and analytics is protected with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • We retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or until you request account deletion.

11 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to accommodate new products, features, or regulatory updates. When revisions occur, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
