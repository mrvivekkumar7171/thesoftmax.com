FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you really collect zero data?

Yes, absolutely. Our applications are serverless — there is no backend server to send data to. The only data stored is in your browser's localStorage for preferences (like volume level), and it never leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why do you need a Privacy Policy if you collect no data?

App stores like Google Play Store and the Apple App Store require a Privacy Policy URL for all app submissions — even if the app collects no data. This page exists to satisfy that requirement and to be transparent about how our apps work.

Can I install your apps on my phone?

Yes! Our apps are built as Progressive Web Apps (PWAs). On Android, open the app in Chrome and tap "Add to Home Screen." On iOS/Safari, tap the share icon and select "Add to Home Screen." The app will behave like a native app.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Use the contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the subject. Please describe what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. Include your browser and device if possible. We'll investigate and fix it as soon as we can.

Are your apps free?

Yes. All current TheSoftMax applications are completely free to use with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. We may add optional features in the future, but the core experience will always remain free.

What technologies do you use?

TheSoftMax apps are built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build tools, no backend. We use the Web APIs available natively in modern browsers, including the Media Session API, Service Worker API, and localStorage. Apps are hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain.
