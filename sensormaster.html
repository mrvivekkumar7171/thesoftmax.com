Diagnostic Capabilities

Precision engineering.
Built for complete device transparency.

Real-time interactive diagnostics covering every critical chip, sensor, transducer, and interface on your Android device.

18 Dedicated Hardware Modules

Comprehensive diagnostic suite evaluating Accelerometer, Gyroscope, Magnetometer, Light, Proximity, CPU, Display, Battery, RAM, Storage, Camera, Speaker, Mic, Haptics, Network, GPS, NFC, and Fingerprint.

165Hz Acoustic Water Ejector

Emits targeted acoustic frequency sweeps and pure low-frequency resonance vibrations specifically calculated to assist in dislodging trapped moisture and particulate dust from speaker grilles.

Display & Multi-Touch Grid

Verify screen health with full-spectrum dead pixel RGB sweeps, 50% gray uniformity inspections, refresh rate detection, touch panel response tracking, and simultaneous multi-finger digitizer tests.

CPU Compute & FPU Stress Test

Measure multi-core and single-core floating-point arithmetic performance in real time. Inspect active CPU core clock speeds, processor architecture, and execution thread stability under load.

Battery Health & Charging Metrics

Monitor real-time battery voltage, charging amperage, chemical health ratings, operating temperatures, power cell technology, and discharge rates with live system broadcast telemetry.

GNSS Orbital Satellite Mesh

Inspect GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, and BeiDou satellite constellation locks. View signal-to-noise ratios, orbital azimuth angles, elevation coordinates, and live geolocation fix precision.

100% On-Device & Private

Your sensor data stays strictly on your phone. No microphone audio samples, camera frames, biometric templates, or precise GPS logs are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to any remote server.

14 Languages & RTL Support

Engineered for a global audience with native support for 14 languages, including English, Japanese, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Traditional Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, and Hebrew with native RTL layout.
